It lives where your apps already are.

There's no window to keep track of. macobar makes a small gap in the Dock right after Finder and puts a real text field in it. Click it, type what you want, press Return.

You stay in charge of your Mac.

An agent that can act on your Mac should be easy to see, easy to stop, and should ask first. That's how Macie is built.

  • Permissions you grant Today

    macobar uses Accessibility to find the Dock and work in your apps, and Screen Recording to see the screen. You allow each one and can turn it off in System Settings.

  • Asks before risky actions Today

    Anything risky waits for your OK. Once she has read a page, file or email in a task, she asks again, even for actions you always allow.

  • A log and a way to stop Today

    Every action is in History, with Undo for file changes. Press Escape in the empty field to stop her.

  • Your key, your account Today

    Your API key stays in your Keychain and goes only to the provider you chose. With a local model, nothing leaves your Mac.

  • Leaves cleanly Today

    Right-click the field and choose Quit macobar. The gap leaves the Dock with it.
