It lives where your apps already are.

There's no window to keep track of. macobar makes a small gap in the Dock right after Finder and puts a real text field in it. Click it, type what you want, press Return.

Where it's headed.

macobar is early and in active development. The field and the reply card are built. Macie, the agent behind them, is what's being built now.

  • A reply card above itRises out of the Dock and streams text as it arrives.

You stay in charge of your Mac.

An agent that can act on your Mac should be easy to see, easy to stop, and should ask first. That's how Macie is being built.

  • Permissions you grant Today

    macobar uses the macOS Accessibility permission, which you allow and can turn off in System Settings. Right now it uses it only to find the Dock.

  • Asks before risky actions Coming

    Anything destructive waits for your OK.

  • A log and a kill switch Coming

    Every action is written down, and one switch stops the agent.

  • Your key, your account Coming

    Bring your own Claude or OpenAI API key, so requests run on your own account.

  • Leaves cleanly Today

    Right-click the field and choose Quit macobar. The gap leaves the Dock with it.
